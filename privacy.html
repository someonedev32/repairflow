Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

RepairBuddy AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

  • Photos and videos you capture or upload for repair analysis
  • Text descriptions of repair issues
  • Repair analysis results and repair history
  • Account information, if you use an account or Sign in with Apple
  • Information you provide when contacting support

b) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Technical information necessary to operate and maintain the App

c) Purchase Information

  • Purchase and transaction identifiers
  • Credit package or product information
  • Purchase status and credit entitlement information
  • App user identifiers used for purchase management

We do not receive your full payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze repair issues using AI models
  • To provide step-by-step repair guidance
  • To identify possible problems from submitted photos, videos, and descriptions
  • To maintain and display your repair history
  • To manage purchases, credits, and access to paid features
  • To improve app performance, reliability, and accuracy
  • To prevent abuse and protect the security of the App
  • To respond to support requests and user inquiries

3. Camera, Photos, and AI Processing

RepairBuddy AI allows you to take photos using your device's camera or select existing photos and videos from your photo library.

The App requests camera access only when you choose to capture an image. Photo library access is used when you choose to import media or save content.

Photos, videos, and descriptions that you submit for repair analysis are uploaded to our servers and may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers to analyze the repair issue and generate repair instructions.

This processing may include analyzing visible objects, damage, symptoms, and other details relevant to the repair request. The submitted media and description are processed for the purpose of providing the requested AI repair analysis.

Do not upload photos, videos, or descriptions containing sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your repair request and you are comfortable submitting it for processing.

4. AI Service Providers

RepairBuddy AI uses third-party AI service providers to process submitted photos, videos, and text descriptions and generate repair guidance.

Information submitted for AI analysis may be transmitted to these providers. They process the information to provide the requested AI functionality and may handle it according to their own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

We do not use submitted repair media for advertising. AI-generated repair guidance is informational and should not be considered a substitute for professional advice, especially for electrical, automotive, structural, or other potentially hazardous repairs.

5. Data Storage

RepairBuddy AI may store repair analysis information and repair history on cloud servers to provide its services and allow you to access previous repair results.

This information may include submitted descriptions, repair analysis results, and related repair history. Photos or videos may be processed or stored as necessary to provide the requested analysis and related functionality.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

The App's AI analysis and cloud-based history features require an internet connection. Camera capture and selecting media are performed through your device, but submitting media for AI analysis requires network access.

6. In-App Purchases and RevenueCat

RepairBuddy AI uses a credit-based purchase system. Users may purchase credit packages to access AI repair analysis and other paid features.

Payments are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your full payment card details.

RevenueCat is used to help manage and verify purchases and credit entitlements. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information such as:

  • App user identifiers
  • Purchase and transaction identifiers
  • Product or credit package information
  • Purchase status and entitlement information
  • Transaction dates and related purchase metadata

This information is used to verify purchases, manage credit entitlements, restore purchases where applicable, and provide access to paid features.

RevenueCat is not used to analyze your repair photos or generate repair instructions. AI media processing is described in Sections 3 and 4.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms. For more information, please review:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

7. Data Sharing

We may share information with the following service providers when necessary to operate the App:

  • AI service providers: to process submitted photos, videos, and descriptions and generate repair analysis.
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers: to store and process repair history and related service information.
  • RevenueCat: to manage and verify in-app purchases and credit entitlements.
  • Apple: to process App Store payments and provide purchase-related information.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for advertising purposes.

If you choose to open a map or contact an external professional through a third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, maintain repair history, manage purchases, resolve support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Repair history and associated information may remain stored on cloud servers until it is deleted or no longer needed for the service.

You may contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal information. We will review and process requests in accordance with applicable law and our operational requirements.

Some purchase records may be retained by Apple or RevenueCat according to their own retention practices and legal or transaction requirements.
